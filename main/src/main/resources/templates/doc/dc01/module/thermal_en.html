Introduction to MODULE_THERMAL Module

Module Description

The product is pre-installed with a thermal imaging module used for capturing thermal images of targets. Thermal imaging sensors are generally expensive devices; this product adopts the most cost-effective sensor, supporting 32*24 pixels.

Thermal imaging differs from regular cameras, which sense visible light. Thermal imaging senses temperature and can be understood as simultaneously measuring the temperature at 32*24 points. From the sensor's perspective, its structure appears as shown in this image. The field of view is cone-like: the closer the object, the smaller the pixel spacing.
