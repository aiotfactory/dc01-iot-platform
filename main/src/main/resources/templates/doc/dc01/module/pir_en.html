MODULE_PIR Introduction

Module Description

The product comes pre-installed with a PIR infrared motion sensor, which can detect the movement or actions of humans or animals within an effective range of approximately 2-3 meters. Upon detecting any movement or action, the sensor triggers the device to report data. The system also supports "coordinated operations", such as taking photos immediately after being triggered.
